🛡️ Safety Committee Meeting at Anish AeroOps

The Safety Committee Meeting is an important part of HSSE and OISD safety management practices conducted regularly at the office and work site to maintain a safe and healthy working environment. The primary objective of the meeting is to bring together management, supervisors, safety officers, contractors, and workers to discuss workplace safety issues, review safety performance, identify hazards, and implement corrective and preventive actions.

The meeting helps improve communication between employees and management regarding safety concerns and encourages active participation in maintaining workplace safety standards. Topics such as accident prevention, unsafe conditions, PPE compliance, housekeeping, toolbox talks, emergency preparedness, work permit systems, and safety observations are discussed during the meeting.

Regular safety committee meetings also help review incident reports, analyze root causes of accidents, evaluate improvements made at the site, and recommend additional safety measures to prevent future incidents. Workers are encouraged to share suggestions, safety concerns, and practical solutions for improving safety practices at the workplace.

The committee also participates in safety awareness programmes, training sessions, mock drills, and information-sharing activities to strengthen the organization’s safety culture. Maintaining records of meetings, attendance, discussions, and recommendations ensures proper monitoring and continuous improvement of safety performance.

  • 📌 Conclusion

    • Safety Committee Meetings play a vital role in creating a proactive safety culture within the organization. Through regular discussions, inspections, training, and teamwork, the committee helps maintain safe working conditions, improve operational safety, and ensure compliance with HSSE and OISD safety standards. Continuous participation and cooperation from all employees are essential for achieving an accident-free and safe workplace.
