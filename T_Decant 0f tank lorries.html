DECANTATION OF TANK LORRIES

VVIP Topic

Purpose

To decant the tank lorry while ensuring that the product received meets specification.

Reference

IP 274 Latest Issue & QHB 01.01.2019

Responsibility

QCS

Documents referred to:

a. Density value reduction factor Chart b. QCRC c. Calibration Certificate. d. Test Report ( Full specification test report / Batch Formation test Report) e. List of Specimen signature of Dispatch location QCS & Test Report Signatories.

🛠️ Tools & Equipment Required

  • Sampling Kit consisting of:
  • 1 Clear Glass Bottle of 1000 ml Capacity.
  • 1 Clear Glass Bottle of 100 ml Capacity.
  • Water Detecting Capsule.
  • Glass Hydrometer scaled in density units of gms/ml conforming to IS: 3104 Part I specifications.
  • Glass Thermometer with temperature range 0–50°C and scale graduation of 0.5°C conforming to IS: 4825 specifications.
  • Hydrometer Jar made of clear glass or clear plastic resistant to fuel discoloration and sunlight exposure.
  • Hydrometer Jar with sufficient width and depth to allow hydrometers to float freely with approximately 2.5 cm clearance.
  • Wiping Material such as Cotton Cloth, Paper Towels, or Cotton Wool.
  • Density / Temperature Chart.
  • Dip Rod.
  • Hose Connecting Spanners.
  • Seals, Sealing Wire & Sealing Pliers.
  • Master Key for Abloy Locks.

Testing Procedure

    1. Check from PDN & SAP whether the subject consignment with correct valuation type is meant for the location.
    2. On receipt of Tank Lorry, switch off the engine and the master battery switch. Keep the vehicle in neutral gear, apply hand brake and place the chokes. Allow the product to settle for a minimum period of 10 minutes.
    3. Bond the Tank Lorry to the earthing point provided at T/L decantation bay to dissipate static charges.
    4. Check the QCRC (Refer AQCAM Appendix-I format) for all relevant details.
    5. Ensure that the Test Report mentioned in the QCRC is available.
    6. Check whether the Tank Lorry is dedicated for ATF transport.
    7. If it is a dedicated Tank Lorry, proceed with decantation.
    8. If not dedicated, verify flushing details from the QCRC before initiating decantation.
    9. Verify all seals for numbers/locks & confirm that they have not been tampered. If seals are found tampered, notify the Location Incharge for appropriate action.
    10. Check calibration and tank cleaning certificates for validity, if required.
    11. Carry out checks as per details given in QCRC (Refer AQCAM Appendix-I).
    12. Open tank foot valves.
    13. Take dips from each compartment after giving sufficient settling time.(Min 10 min)
    14. Drain line quantity and approximately 5 litres of product from each compartment.
    15. Draw sample from each compartment and perform Test “G” and “H”.
    16. If water or sediments are observed, repeat last two Steps
    17. Maintain written record for water and cleanliness check in QC Check Form (Mobile) as per QHB Format No. Q-6.
    18. Check that the Hydrometer and Thermometer at site are appropriate and undamaged.
    19. Note the density at observed temperature and convert to density at 15°C using Density Value Reduction Factor Chart.
    20. Correlate the observed density at 15°C with the dispatch density at 15°C mentioned in the QCRC.
    21. A variation of not more than ± 2.5 Kg/m³ is allowed.
    22. If the variation exceeds ± 2.5 Kg/m³, carry out a re-test to confirm accuracy.
    23. If the product fails the re-test, inform the Location Incharge as a likely “Non Conforming Product” and await instructions.
    24. If the product passes the re-test, accept the product.
    25. Enter all details in the QCRC along with deviation, if any.
    26. In case of deviation, communicate the same to the dispatch location.
    27. Check the receipt line to ensure that product will go into the designated tank having adequate ullage.
    28. Remove blanks on the decantation hoses and connect the hoses to the Tank Lorry before starting decantation. Ensure cone filters are intact in the hose.
    29. After completion of decantation, re-fit the blanks on the decantation hose.
    30. Physically check the Tank Lorry to ensure that it is completely empty.
    31. Seal/Lock the Tank Lorry compartments and manifold before sending it back.
    32. After completion of receipt into the tank, allow stipulated settling time.(Min 1 hrs)
    33. Test the product for presence of water, sediments, and appearance.
    34. Release the product for delivery only if samples are clear and free from sediments and water.
    35. Maintain written record in QC Check Form (Static) as per QHB Format No. Q-8.

Important Note

The ATF product is accepted only after successful completion of all quality control and inspection procedures. The product must be clear, bright, and free from water, sediments, or any contamination. The observed density at 15°C should match the dispatch density within the permissible variation limit of ± 2.5 Kg/m³. All seals, documents, and QC records must be verified and found satisfactory before decantation and release for delivery. Adequate settling time must be provided at different stages of the operation, including a minimum settling period of 10 minutes after receipt of the Tank Lorry and stipulated settling time after product receipt into the storage tank before testing and release.

If any abnormality such as seal tampering, contamination, excessive density variation, or failed re-test is observed, the product shall be treated as a Non-Conforming Product and immediately reported to the Location Incharge for further action and instructions.
