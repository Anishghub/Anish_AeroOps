⛑️ SAFETY PLEDGE(सुरक्षा शपथ)🦺

I will observe and follow all procedures and practices.

I will ensure that I have understood procedures before starting an activity, job assigned to me.

I will ensure that all critical safety systems are in line at my work area.

I will not by-pass any control system.

I will not allow deviation from laid down operating parameters and correct the deviations.

I will intervene in case I observe any unsafe behavior.

I will participate and understand effectively in training to be competent in the work.

I will wear personal protective equipment as appropriate.

I will understand emergency response plan, developed from potential emergency scenarios before commencement of work.

“Your Safety is Our Highest Responsibility.”
