COPPER CORROSION TEST

Frequency: Monthly

Purpose

To check extent of corrosiveness of Jet A-1 using a copper strip.

Reference

IS 1448 (P15) Latest Issue

Responsibility

QC In-charge / QC Signatory

🛠️ Tools & Equipment Required

  • Glass Test Tubes – 25 mm × 150 mm.
  • Stainless Steel Bombs.
  • Tall Glass Beaker.
  • Water Bath capable of maintaining temperature of 100 ± 1 °C.
  • Thermometer of adequate range with scale graduation of 0.5°C and support to hold it vertically.
  • Iso-octane.
  • Strip Vice and Polishing Materials including Silicon Carbide Paper of varying fineness including 240 Grade Paper or Cloth, 150 Mesh Silicon Carbide Grain, and Pharmaceutical Grade Absorbent Cotton Wool.
  • Filter Paper.
  • Stainless Steel Forceps.
  • Copper Strips of Standard Size.

Testing Procedure

  1. Draw a two litres tank bottom sample from the tank from which product is to be tested in a clean aluminum container / Stained glass container.
  2. Surface preparation: Remove all surface blemishes from all six sides of the strip, holding it with ashless filter paper and rubbing it in a rotary motion on a sheet of abrasive paper moistened with iso-octane and kept on a flat surface. Immerse the strip in iso octane before final polishing.
  3. Final Polishing: Remove a strip from the iso-octane. Placing it in the strip vice, polish first the ends and then the sides with 150-mesh silicon carbide grains picked up from a clean glass plate with a pad of cotton moistened with iso-octane. Rub in the direction of long axis of the strip, carrying the stroke beyond the end of the strip before reversing the direction. Clean all metal dust from the strip with thin cotton pads until a fresh cotton pad remains unsoiled. Immerse the strip in the sample within 1 minute of final polishing.
  4. Pour 30 ml of sample, completely clear and free of any suspended water into a clean dry test tube.
  5. Within 1 min. of complete polishing, slide the copper strip into the sample tube, insert the tube in the stainless steel bomb and screw the lid on tight.
  6. Completely immerse the bomb in a boiling water bath at 100 1 °C.
  7. After 2 hrs. 5 min. withdraw the bomb and immerse it in tap water for a few minutes.
  8. Open the bomb and empty the contents of test tube into a tall glass beaker, letting the strip gently slide out.
  9. Immediately immerse the strip in a wash solvent (Refer Clause 4.1 of IS 1448(P:15) –Latest Issue ), withdraw the strip using stainless steel forceps and dry with quantitative filter paper.
  10. Compare the strip with ASTM Copper Strip Standard chart. Care should be taken to prevent exposure of excessive sunlight on the ASTM Copper Strip Standard Chart.
  11. The report should not be worse than 1, i.e. Slight tarnish or almost the same as a freshly polished strip.
  12. Record the result in a "Corrosion Test Report " as per the format placed in Format No. Q-4 in QHB.

Important Note

The Silver Strip Corrosion Test result is considered satisfactory when the silver strip rating is not worse than “1” as per IP 227 Latest Issue standards. After completion of the test at 50 ± 1 °C for four hours, the silver strip should exhibit only slight tarnish, such as faint brown or white discoloration.
