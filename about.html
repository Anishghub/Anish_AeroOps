About Me..

Hello! 👋 I’m Anish Kumar, an Assistant Aviation Officer with professional experience in aviation fuel operations, quality control, operational management, safety compliance, and technology-enabled processes.

I have experience working with leading Oil & Gas PSUs including IOCL, HPCL, and AAO, handling critical aviation fuel operations such as Jet A-1 fuel handling, into-plane refueling, tank farm operations, fuel quality testing, refueller management, inventory control, and flight fueling coordination.

My role also involves ensuring DGCA and statutory compliance, maintaining operational and regulatory documentation, managing SAP records, supporting automated fuel handling systems, and monitoring critical equipment and processes to maintain safe, reliable, and efficient operations.

I am a responsible, safety-focused, and adaptable professional with strong skills in team coordination, communication, problem-solving, decision-making, time management, and multitasking. I also have technical knowledge in Java, Python, JavaScript, SQL, React.js, Power BI, Advanced Excel, SAP, and IT support, which helps me contribute to process improvement and technology-driven operational efficiency.

I am passionate about maintaining high operational standards, continuously improving processes, and contributing to organizations where safety, quality, efficiency, and teamwork are valued.

✈️ Aviation Fuel Operations & Quality Assurance

Managing safe, reliable, and efficient Jet A-1 / ATF aircraft refuelling operations while ensuring strict compliance with aviation safety, fuel quality, regulatory, and operational procedures.

🛫 Aircraft Refuelling Operations

Executing domestic and international aircraft refuelling operations with proper vehicle positioning, bonding/grounding, nozzle connection, communication, safety precautions, and adherence to approved operating procedures.

🧪 Fuel Quality Control & QCS

Performing Quality Control & Sampling (QCS) activities including fuel sampling, water detection, visual inspection, contamination checks, filter/strainer monitoring, and maintaining fuel quality in accordance with applicable aviation fuel standards and procedures.

🔍 Pre-Operational Safety Checks

Conducting comprehensive checks of refuellers, hydrant dispensers, hoses, nozzles, bonding cables, emergency shutdown systems, fire extinguishers, safety equipment, and operational tools before and during fuelling operations.

💻 Digital Systems & IT Support

Supporting SAP, digital operational systems, Excel-based reporting, data analysis, hardware/software troubleshooting, LAN/network support, and automation initiatives to improve operational efficiency and data-driven decision-making.
