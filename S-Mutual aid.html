    • 🤝 Mutual Aid Meeting & Emergency Coordination

    A Quarterly Mutual Aid Meeting was conducted among various Oil Marketing Companies (OMCs) and airport emergency agencies to strengthen coordination, emergency preparedness, and disaster management capabilities during emergency situations and mock drills. Representatives from organizations including Indian Oil Corporation Limited, Hindustan Petroleum Corporation Limited, Anish AeroOps Corporation Limited, Chandigarh International Airport Limited, BSSPL, CISF, BCAS, IAF Chandigarh, and medical emergency teams participated in the meeting.

    During the meeting, important discussions were held regarding safety precautions during the monsoon season, equipment and infrastructure availability, Disaster Management Plan (DMP) drills, First Aid Training programmes, airport biometric pass issues, and coordination with Factories Department for conducting emergency mock drills at Aviation Fuel Stations (AFS).

    As part of the Disaster Control Management Plan (DCMP), a Level-II Mock Fire Drill under an oil fire scenario was organized at AAO Anish AeroOps with active participation from mutual aid agencies and airport emergency services. The objective of the drill was to improve coordination, communication, emergency response capability, firefighting operations, rescue preparedness, and resource sharing during major emergencies.

    • 📌 Conclusion

    Mutual Aid participation plays a vital role in improving emergency preparedness and disaster management during critical situations. Joint coordination among OMCs, airport authorities, fire services, medical teams, and security agencies helps ensure quick response, effective resource utilization, and better control of emergencies. Regular mutual aid meetings and mock drills strengthen teamwork, operational safety, and overall emergency response capability within aviation and industrial operations.
